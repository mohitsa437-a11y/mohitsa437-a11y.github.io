Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Meme Bot ("the app") is a personal, single-user desktop tool. Its only user is the owner of the YouTube channel and Instagram account it publishes to.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google API Services): with the owner's permission, the app uploads the owner's own videos (youtube.upload) and reads basic information and statistics about the owner's own channel and videos, such as view, like and comment counts (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly).
  • Instagram: with the owner's permission, the app publishes the owner's own Reels and reads insights (views, likes, shares) for those posts.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to decide the best time to post.
  • Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The app does not access data of any other users.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) and in Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites. Deleting the app's folder removes all locally stored data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google consent screen.
